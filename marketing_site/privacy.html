1. Introduction & Core Philosophy

Welcome to Hisabify ("we", "our", or "the app"), accessible at hisabify.aadarshapandit.com.np and through our web application at app.hisabify.aadarshapandit.com.np.

Hisabify was built by Aadarsh Pandit in Nepal to solve the everyday hassle of splitting flat, grocery, and trip expenses fairly without awkwardness. We believe financial transparency should never come at the expense of your privacy. We do not sell your personal data, we do not run third-party tracker advertisements, and we collect only the minimal information needed to calculate shared debts accurately.

📋

2. Information We Collect

To provide accurate expense splitting and real-time synchronization between flatmates, we collect:

  • Account Information: When you sign in (via Google or Email authentication), we store your public display name, email address, and unique user identifier (UID) managed securely via Google Firebase.
  • Room & Membership Details: Room names, custom icons/emojis, 6-character room invitation codes, and member roles (Admin or Flatmate).
  • Expense & Debt Records: Expense titles, amounts, categories, payer name, who is included in the split, soft-delete audit records, and settlement transactions between members.
  • Local Device Cache: Preferences like your last active room and UI states stored locally in your browser/device for instant zero-latency loading.
💡 We Never Collect: Bank account credentials, credit card numbers, government IDs, physical address coordinates, or biometric information. Hisabify is a tracker and calculator, not a payment gateway.
⚡

3. How Your Information is Used

The information we collect is strictly utilized for the following core purposes:

  • Authenticating your session and connecting you to your joined flats/rooms.
  • Calculating running balances, per-person equal shares, and debt minimization settlements.
  • Providing real-time updates to all room members whenever an expense is logged, edited, or settled.
  • Maintaining transparent audit trails (e.g., showing who recorded or soft-deleted an expense) to prevent secret modifications.
🛡️

4. Data Security & Storage

All Hisabify data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore infrastructure adhering to modern industry-standard security protocols. Data transmissions are encrypted in transit using SSL/TLS encryption (HTTPS).

Database reads and writes are guarded by strict Firestore security rules requiring authentication so only verified members can view or manage expenses within their shared rooms.

🤝

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal or financial data. We only share information with trusted infrastructure service providers strictly required to operate the app:

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud: For user authentication, cloud database storage, and web application hosting.
  • Vercel: For fast global delivery and static hosting of our official landing site.
🗑️

6. Data Ownership & Your Rights

You retain complete ownership over your data. You have the right to:

  • Leave any room or group whenever you wish.
  • Soft-delete or permanently purge incorrect expense entries (admins).
  • Request complete account deletion and erasure of your associated profile by contacting us directly.
📬
