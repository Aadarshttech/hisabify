Privacy Policy

Transparency first. Learn how Hisabify protects your data, calculates shared debts, and respects your privacy.

← Back to Hisabify Overview
volunteer_activism

Built for People, Not Advertisers

Hisabify is an open financial collaboration utility created by Aadarsh Pandit in Nepal. We believe settling shared roommate debts should be stress-free, accurate, and completely private. We do not sell your personal details, run tracking ads, or profit off your data.

database 1. What Information We Collect
+

We only store the minimal information required to compute balances and synchronize shared room ledgers:

  • Account Profile: Display name, email address, and unique user ID provided when you authenticate via Google or Email.
  • Room Configuration: Room names, custom emojis/icons, 6-character room codes, and role permissions (Admin or Member).
  • Expenses & Payments: Amount, title, category, who paid, who shared in the expense, timestamps, and settlement records.
  • Local Preferences: Stored locally on your device (e.g. cached room selection) to provide instant load speeds.
lock
Zero Financial Credentials: Hisabify never collects bank account numbers, passwords, debit/credit cards, or national IDs. We calculate balances—we never handle banking transactions.
sync_alt 2. How Your Information Is Used
+

Your data is used strictly to power the core features of the Hisabify application:

  • Calculating equal and customized per-person splits without manual mathematics.
  • Running our debt-minimization algorithm so flatmates settle up in the fewest possible payments.
  • Real-time database sync across all flatmate devices using Cloud Firestore.
  • Maintaining transparent audit trails with soft-deletion indicators so no charges can be erased secretly.
shield 3. Security & Infrastructure
+

We host our cloud backend on Google Cloud Platform / Firebase in high-availability enterprise data centers:

  • All data transmissions are protected via SSL/TLS encryption (HTTPS).
  • Database queries are guarded by role-based Cloud Firestore Security Rules, ensuring only authenticated members can access room details.
  • Authentication tokens are handled directly by Google Identity Services.
handshake 4. Third-Party Sharing & Advertisers
+

We do not sell, license, or monetize your data with marketing agencies or data brokers. The only external services involved are infrastructure providers essential to delivering the web application:

  • Google Firebase: Cloud database, authentication, and application hosting.
  • Vercel: Global content delivery network (CDN) for fast landing page delivery.
manage_accounts 5. Your Rights & Account Deletion
+

You have full ownership of your presence on Hisabify:

  • You may switch between rooms or leave any flat at any time.
  • Admins can soft-delete erroneous transactions or permanently purge old records.
  • To request permanent deletion of your user profile and associated account data, email us directly and we will process the erasure promptly.
